现在按照式 (9.2.26) 变换到广义坐标系  $ P(q_{1}, q_{2}, q_{3}) $ 和  $ Q(q_{1} + \mathrm{d}q_{1}, q_{2} + \mathrm{d}q_{2}, q_{3} + \mathrm{d}q_{3}) $，则有

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{r}{\mathrm{d}x=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}}\\ {\mathrm{d}y=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}}\\ {\mathrm{d}z=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3}\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}}\end{array}\right\} $$ 

所以

 $$ \begin{aligned}(\mathrm{d}s)^{2}=&\left(\sum_{i}\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{j}\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_{j}}\mathrm{d}q_{j}\right)+\left(\sum_{i}\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{j}\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_{j}}\mathrm{d}q_{j}\right)\\&+\left(\sum_{i}\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_{i}}\mathrm{d}q_{i}\right)\left(\sum_{j}\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_{j}}\mathrm{d}q_{j}\right)\end{aligned} $$ 

对于正交坐标系，式 (9.2.34) 中任何  $ i \neq j $ 的交错项都等于零，这是因为微分矢量  $ \mathrm{d}q_{1} $， $ \mathrm{d}q_{2} $， $ \mathrm{d}q_{3} $ 彼此垂直，所以

 $$ \mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{q}_{i}\cdot\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{q}_{j}=0\qquad(i\neq j;i,j=1,2,3) $$ 

于是式(9.2.34)简化为

 $$ \left(\mathrm{d}s\right)^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{i}^{2}+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{i}^{2}+\sum_{i=1}^{3}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{i}^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}h_{i}^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{i}^{2} $$ 

式中

 $$ h_{i}^{2}=\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial q_{i}}\right)^{2}\qquad(i=1,2,3) $$ 

如果交错项不等于零，即不满足式（9.2.35）的坐标系称为非正交坐标系.

如果将式(9.2.36)写为

 $$ (\mathrm{d}s)^{2}=(\mathrm{d}s_{1})^{2}+(\mathrm{d}s_{2})^{2}+(\mathrm{d}s_{3})^{2}=h_{1}^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{1}^{2}+h_{2}^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{2}^{2}+h_{3}^{2}\mathrm{d}q_{3}^{2} $$ 

则有

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l}{\mathrm{d}s_{1}=h_{1}\mathrm{d}q_{1}}\\ {\mathrm{d}s_{2}=h_{2}\mathrm{d}q_{2}}\\ {\mathrm{d}s_{3}=h_{3}\mathrm{d}q_{3}}\end{array}\right\} $$
