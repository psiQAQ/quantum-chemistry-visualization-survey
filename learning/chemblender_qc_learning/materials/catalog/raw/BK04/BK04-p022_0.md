式（9.2.38）或式（9.2.39）称为正交广义坐标系的距离公式

(3) 球坐标系中的距离. 在球坐标系中,  $ q_{1}=r $,  $ q_{2}=\theta $,  $ q_{3}=\phi $, 则有

 $$ \begin{aligned}h_{1}^{2}&=h_{r}^{2}=\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial r}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial r}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial r}\right)^{2}\\&=\sin^{2}\theta\cos^{2}\phi+\sin^{2}\theta\sin^{2}\phi+\cos^{2}\theta\\&=\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta=1\\ \end{aligned} $$ 

所以

 $$ h_{r}=1 $$ 

同理

 $$ \begin{aligned}h_{2}^{2}&=h_{\theta}^{2}=\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial\theta}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial\theta}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial\theta}\right)^{2}\\&=r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta\cos^{2}\phi+r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta\sin^{2}\phi+r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta\\&=r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta+r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta=r^{2}\\ \end{aligned} $$ 

所以

 $$ h_{\theta}=r $$ 

 $$ \begin{aligned}h_{3}^{2}&=h_{\phi}^{2}=\left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial\phi}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial\phi}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial\phi}\right)^{2}\\&=r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta\sin^{2}\phi+r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta\cos^{2}\phi=r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta\end{aligned} $$ 

所以

 $$ \left.\begin{aligned}{}&{{}h_{\phi}=r\mathrm{s i n}\theta}\\ {}&{{}\mathrm{d}s_{1}=h_{1}\mathrm{d}q_{1}=h_{r}\mathrm{d}r=\mathrm{d}r}\\ {}&{{}\mathrm{d}s_{2}=h_{2}\mathrm{d}q_{2}=h_{\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=r\mathrm{d}\theta}\\ {}&{{}\mathrm{d}s_{3}=h_{3}\mathrm{d}q_{3}=h_{\phi}\mathrm{d}\phi=r\mathrm{s i n}\theta\mathrm{d}\phi}\\ \end{aligned}\right\} $$ 

(4) 广义坐标系中的体积元  $ \mathrm{d}\tau $. 在直角坐标系中

 $$ \mathrm{d}\tau=\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\mathrm{d}z $$ 

在正交广义坐标系中

 $$ \mathrm{d}\tau=\mathrm{d}s_{1}\mathrm{d}s_{2}\mathrm{d}s_{3}=h_{1}h_{2}h_{3}\mathrm{d}q_{1}\mathrm{d}q_{2}\mathrm{d}q_{3} $$ 

例如，在球坐标系中有

 $$ \mathrm{d}\tau=r^{2}\mathrm{s i n}\theta\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\theta\mathrm{d}\phi $$
