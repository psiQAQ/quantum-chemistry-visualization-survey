和

 $$ \frac{\partial}{\partial q_{3}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{1}h_{2}}{h_{3}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{3}}\right)\mathrm{d}q_{1}\mathrm{d}q_{2}\mathrm{d}q_{3} $$ 

所以在体积元  $ d\tau $ 内总的质量积累速度等于以上三式之和，又因为

 $$ \mathrm{d}\tau=\mathrm{d}s_{1}\mathrm{d}s_{2}\mathrm{d}s_{3}=h_{1}h_{2}h_{3}\mathrm{d}q_{1}\mathrm{d}q_{2}\mathrm{d}q_{3} $$ 

故在体积元  $ \mathrm{d}\tau $ 内密度增加的速度为

 $$ \frac{\partial\rho}{\mathrm{d}t}=\frac{1}{h_{1}h_{2}h_{3}}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{1}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{2}h_{3}}{h_{1}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{1}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{2}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{1}h_{3}}{h_{2}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{2}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{3}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{1}h_{2}}{h_{3}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{3}}\right)\right] $$ 

如果在直角坐标系中作类似上面的分析，我们得到

 $$ \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\rho\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\rho\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(\rho\frac{\partial v}{\partial z}\right)=\nabla\cdot(\rho\nabla v) $$ 

因为  $ \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} $ 与坐标的选择无关，所以

 $$ \mathbf{\nabla}\cdot\left(\rho\mathbf{\nabla}v\right)=\frac{1}{h_{1}h_{2}h_{3}}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{1}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{2}h_{3}}{h_{1}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{1}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{2}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{1}h_{3}}{h_{2}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{2}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{3}}\left(\rho\frac{h_{1}h_{2}}{h_{3}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{3}}\right)\right] $$ 

当考虑  $ \rho = $ 常数的特例，并注意  $ \nabla \cdot \nabla = \nabla^2 $，得

 $$ \nabla^{2}=\frac{1}{h_{1}h_{2}h_{3}}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{1}}\left(\frac{h_{2}h_{3}}{h_{1}}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{1}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{2}}\left(\frac{h_{1}h_{3}}{h_{2}}\frac{\partial v}{\partial q_{2}}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{3}}\left(\frac{h_{1}h_{2}}{h_{3}}\frac{\partial}{\partial q_{3}}\right)\right] $$ 

式（9.2.54）就是 Laplace 算符在正交广义坐标系的表示式．例如，球坐标系

 $$ \begin{aligned}{}&{{}q_{1}=r\quad}&{q_{2}=\theta\quad}&{{}\quad q_{3}=\phi}\\ {}&{{}h_{1}=1\quad}&{h_{2}=r\quad}&{{}\quad h_{3}=r\mathbf{s}\mathbf{i}\mathbf{n}\theta}\\ \end{aligned} $$ 

代入式(9.2.54)，即得

 $$ \nabla^{2}\psi=\frac{1}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left(r^{2}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r}\right)+\frac{1}{r^{2}\sin\theta}\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\left(\sin\theta\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial\theta}\right)+\frac{1}{r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta}\frac{\partial^{2}\psi}{\partial\phi^{2}}. $$ 

这就是前面通过繁杂的偏微商运算得到的式(9.2.25).

#### 9.2.5 椭圆坐标系

除前面所述的直角坐标系和球坐标系外，还有其他的正交曲线坐标系，其中在量子化学中也常用的是椭圆坐标系。这是因为在分子中，价电子常属于相邻的两个原子。用这两个原子核作为焦点的椭圆坐标系对计算有关分子积分比较方便。
