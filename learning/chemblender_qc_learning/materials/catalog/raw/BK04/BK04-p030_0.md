所以

 $$ A_{m}=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-|m|)!}{(l+|m|)!}\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{d}\Omega_{1} $$ 

式（9.3.15）中包含  $ P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})e^{-im\phi_{1}} $ 的积分，仍然很麻烦，需要进一步处理.

第三步，将  $  P_l^{|m|} (\cos\theta_1) e^{-im\phi_1}  $ 展开

 $$ P_{l}^{|m|}(\mathrm{c o s}\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}=\sum_{k^{\prime}=-l}^{l}B_{k^{\prime}}P_{l}^{|k^{\prime}|}(\mathrm{c o s}\theta)\mathrm{e}^{i k^{\prime}\phi} $$ 

为了求  $ B_{k'} $，对式 (9.3.16) 乘以

 $$ P_{l}^{|k|}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{e}^{-i k\phi}\mathrm{d}\Omega\qquad(\mathrm{d}\Omega=\sin\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\mathrm{d}\phi) $$ 

并积分，得

 $$ \begin{aligned}&\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}^{|k|}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{e}^{-i k\phi}\mathrm{d}\varOmega\\ =&\sum_{k^{\prime}=-l}^{l}B_{k^{\prime}}\int P_{l}^{|k^{\prime}|}(\cos\theta)P_{l}^{|k|}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{e}^{i(k^{\prime}-k)\phi}\mathrm{d}\varOmega\\ =&\left\{\begin{array}{l}0\quad( 当 k^{\prime}\neq k)\\ B_{k}\int_{-1}^{+1}[P_{l}^{|k|}(\cos\theta)]^{2}\mathrm{d}\cos\theta\int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\phi\end{array}\right.\\ =&B_{k}\left[\frac{2}{2l+1}\frac{(l+|k|)!}{(l-|k|)!}2\pi\right]\end{aligned} $$ 

所以

 $$ B_{k}=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-|k|)!}{(l+|k|)!}\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\operatorname{c o s}\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}^{|k|}(\operatorname{c o s}\theta)\mathrm{e}^{-i k\phi}\mathrm{d}\Omega $$ 

对于 k=0 ，有

 $$ B_{0}=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{d}\Omega $$ 

由式（9.3.15）可知

 $$ A_{m}=\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-|m|)!}{(l+|m|)!}\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{d}\Omega $$ 

比较上述两式，得

 $$ A_{m}=\frac{(l-|m|)!}{(l+|m|)!}B_{0} $$
