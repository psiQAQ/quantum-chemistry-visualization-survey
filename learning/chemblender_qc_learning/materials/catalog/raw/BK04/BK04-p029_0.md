(2) 球谐函数的加法公式. 为了将展开式 (9.3.1) 中  $ P_{l}(\cos\theta) $ 表示成包含  $ \theta_{1} $ 和  $ \theta_{2} $ (参见图 9.3.1) 的式子, 需要用到球谐函数的加法公式, 即

 $$ P_{l}(\cos\theta)=\sum_{n=-l}^{l}\frac{(l-|m|)!}{(l+|m|)!}P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{2})\mathrm{e}^{i m(\phi_{1}-\phi_{2})} $$ 

证明 第一步，证明

 $$ \cos\theta=\cos\theta_{1}\cos\theta_{2}+\sin\theta_{1}\sin\theta_{2}\cos(\phi_{1}-\phi_{2}) $$ 

考虑

 $$ \begin{aligned}\boldsymbol{r}_{1}\cdot\boldsymbol{r}_{2}=&r_{1}r_{2}\mathrm{cos}\theta=x_{1}x_{2}+y_{1}y_{2}+z_{1}z_{2}\\=&(r_{1}\mathrm{sin}\theta_{1}\mathrm{cos}\phi_{1})(r_{2}\mathrm{sin}\theta_{2}\mathrm{cos}\phi_{2})+(r_{1}\mathrm{sin}\theta_{1}\mathrm{sin}\phi_{1})(r_{2}\mathrm{sin}\theta_{2}\mathrm{sin}\phi_{2})\\&+(r_{1}\mathrm{cos}\theta_{1})(r_{2}\mathrm{cos}\theta_{2})\end{aligned} $$ 

两边消去  $ r_{1} $ 和  $ r_{2} $，则得

 $$ \cos\theta=\sin\theta_{1}\sin\theta_{2}\cos(\phi_{1}-\phi_{2})+\cos\theta_{1}\cos\theta_{2} $$ 

第二步，将  $ P_{l}(\cos\theta) $ 展开

 $$ P_{l}(\cos\theta)=\sum_{m^{\prime}=-l}^{l}A_{m^{\prime}}P_{l}^{|m^{\prime}|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{i m^{\prime}\phi_{1}} $$ 

为了求系数  $ A_{m'} $，对式 (9.3.13) 两边乘以

 $$ P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}\mathrm{d}\Omega_{1}\qquad(\mathrm{d}\Omega_{1}=\sin\theta_{1}\mathrm{d}\theta_{1}\mathrm{d}\phi_{1}) $$ 

再积分，得

 $$ \begin{aligned}&\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{-i m\phi_{1}}P_{l}(\cos\theta)\mathrm{d}\Omega_{1}\\=&\sum_{m^{\prime}=-l}^{l}A_{m^{\prime}}\int P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})P_{l}^{|m^{\prime}|}(\cos\theta_{1})\mathrm{e}^{i(m^{\prime}-m)\phi_{1}}\mathrm{d}\Omega_{1}\\=&\left\{\begin{array}{l}0\quad( 当 m^{\prime}\neq m)\\A_{m}\int_{-1}^{+1}[P_{l}^{|m|}(\cos\theta_{1})]^{2}\mathrm{d}\cos\theta_{1}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\phi_{1}\end{array}\right.\\=&A_{m}\left[\frac{2}{2l+1}\frac{(l+|m|)!}{(l-|m|)!}2\pi\right]\end{aligned} $$
