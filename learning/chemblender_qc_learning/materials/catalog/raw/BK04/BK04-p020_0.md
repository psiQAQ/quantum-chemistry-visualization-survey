#### 9.2.3 广义坐标系

(1) 直角坐标系  $ (x, y, z) $ 与广义坐标系  $ (q_{1}, q_{2}, q_{3}) $ 的变换关系为

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l}{x=x(q_{1},q_{2},q_{3})}\\ {y=y(q_{1},q_{2},q_{3})}\\ {z=z(q_{1},q_{2},q_{3})}\end{array}\right\} $$ 

逆变换为

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l}{q_{1}=q_{1}(x,y,z)}\\ {q_{2}=q_{2}(x,y,z)}\\ {q_{3}=q_{3}(x,y,z)}\end{array}\right\} $$ 

例如，在球坐标系中， $ q_{1}=r $， $ q_{2}=\theta $， $ q_{3}=\phi $，则有

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l}{x=r\mathrm{s i n}\theta\mathrm{c o s}\phi}\\ {y=r\mathrm{s i n}\theta\mathrm{s i n}\phi}\\ {z=r\mathrm{c o s}\theta}\end{array}\right\} $$ 

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l} r = \sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2}} \\ \theta = \cos^{-1} \left( \frac{z}{\sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2}}} \right) \\ \phi = \tan^{-1} \frac{y}{x} \end{array}\right\} $$ 

又如，在圆柱坐标系中， $ q_{1}=r $， $ q_{2}=\theta $，q3=z，则有

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l}{x=r\mathrm{{c o s}\theta}}\\ {y=r\mathrm{{s i n}\theta}}\\ {z=z}\end{array}\right\} $$ 

 $$ \left.\begin{array}{l} r = \sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}} \\ \theta = \tan^{-1}\frac{y}{x} \\ z = z \end{array}\right\} $$ 

(2) 广义坐标系中的距离. 在直角坐标系中, 点  $ P(x, y, z) $ 与  $ Q(x + \mathrm{d}x, y + \mathrm{d}y, z + \mathrm{d}z) $ 的距离 ds 可表示为

 $$ \left(\mathrm{d}s\right)^{2}=\left(\mathrm{d}x\right)^{2}+\left(\mathrm{d}y\right)^{2}+\left(\mathrm{d}z\right)^{2} $$
