微商式(9.2.9)的第一式，可得

 $$ \frac{\partial r}{\partial x}=\frac{x}{r}\qquad\frac{\partial r}{\partial y}=\frac{y}{r}\qquad\frac{\partial r}{\partial z}=\frac{z}{r} $$ 

 $$ \left.\begin{aligned}&\frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial x^{2}}=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{x}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial r}{\partial x}=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{x^{2}}{r^{3}}\\ &\frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial y^{2}}=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{y^{2}}{r^{3}}\\ &\frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial z^{2}}=\frac{1}{r}-\frac{z^{2}}{r^{3}}\end{aligned}\right\} $$ 

所以

 $$ \left(\frac{\partial r}{\partial\overrightarrow{x}}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial r}{\partial y}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\partial r}{\partial z}\right)^{2}=\frac{1}{r^{2}}(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2})=1 $$ 

 $$ \frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial x^{2}}+\frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial y^{2}}+\frac{\partial^{2}r}{\partial z^{2}}=\frac{3}{r}-\frac{1}{r^{3}}(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2})=\frac{2}{r} $$ 

微商式(9.2.9)的第二式，可得

 $$ \sec^{2}\theta\frac{\partial\theta}{\partial x}=\frac{x}{z\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}} $$ 

所以
